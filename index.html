How Transformers Think:
Interactive Multi-Head Self-Attention

Self-Attention is the mechanism that allows an LLM to dynamically connect words across a sentence. Explore how Queries ($Q$), Keys ($K$), and Values ($V$) compute scaled dot products, why changing a single adjective redirects pronouns (Winograd Schema), how Induction Circuits copy patterns in-context, and how Causal Masking prevents peeking into the future.

🐾 Winograd Resolution: "tired" $\to$ animal vs "wide" $\to$ street
⚡ Induction Heads: In-context copying circuits $[A][B] \dots [A] \to [B]$
🔒 Causal Masking: Upper-triangular $-\infty$ prevents future leakage
📐 Scaled Dot Product: $\text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$

The Complete Transformer Attention Pipeline

How input embeddings pass through $W_Q, W_K, W_V$ projection weights to produce context-aware representations:

01

Input Vectors ($X$)

Tokens enter as static or previous layer embedding vectors $X \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d_{\text{model}}}$. At this stage, tokens have no information about their surrounding sentence.

$$\vec{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}}}$$
02

Linear Projections ($Q, K, V$)

$X$ is multiplied by three learned weight matrices: $W_Q$ (Queries: what am I looking for?), $W_K$ (Keys: what do I contain?), and $W_V$ (Values: what information do I pass on?).

$$Q = XW_Q, \quad K = XW_K, \quad V = XW_V$$
03

Scaled Dot Product

Compute compatibility matrix $QK^T$. To prevent dot products from growing excessively large with high dimension $d_k$ (which causes vanishing softmax gradients), divide by $\sqrt{d_k}$.

$$\text{Scores} = \frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}$$
04

Softmax & Causal Masking

In GPT autoregressive models, upper-triangular positions are set to $-\infty$ so tokens cannot look ahead. Softmax normalizes each row into a probability distribution summing to $1.000$.

$$\alpha = \text{softmax}\left(\text{Scores} + M\right)$$
05

Weighted Value Sum ($O$)

Multiply attention weights $\alpha$ by the Value vectors $V$. Each word's new representation is a weighted average of all words it attended to!

$$\vec{o}_i = \sum_{j=1}^N \alpha_{ij} \vec{v}_j$$
06

Multi-Head Fusion ($W_O$)

Outputs from all $h$ parallel attention heads are concatenated and multiplied by output matrix $W_O$, fusing syntactic, semantic, and induction perspectives together.

$$\text{MHA} = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h)W_O$$
